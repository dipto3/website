Capabilities Matrix

Our Complete Buying House Scope

From initial costing consumption sheets to container loading at Chittagong Port.

Costing & Consumption

We perform precise fabric consumption and component costing for any specific style. By matching with optimal specialized factories, we confirm the most competitive price without sacrificing durability or safety compliance.

  • Open-book cost breakdown
  • 24 to 48-hour rapid turnaround

Sampling & Prototyping

Our dedicated development merchandisers craft Development Samples, Fit Samples, Red Seal, and Gold Seal approval pieces. Our QC team checks every stitch against the technical file before international dispatch.

  • Rapid proto dispatch in 5-7 days
  • Measurement tolerance audits (±0.5cm)

Production Merchandising

Upon Master LC or TT confirmation, our production merchandiser locks fabric and trims bookings, convenes pre-production planning meetings with factory lines, and monitors daily output velocity against critical path milestones.

  • Dedicated point of contact
  • Daily tracking of spinning & weaving

Laboratory Testing

In-house and third-party accredited testing (SGS, Intertek, Bureau Veritas). We evaluate bulk fabric hangers, shade continuity, lab dip delta values, color rub fastness, pilling resistance, and dimensional stability.

  • Formal lab test certificate with shipment
  • Zero toxic chemicals (REACH compliant)

4-Stage Quality Audits

We execute inline inspections continuously during production, pre-production checks (at 15% bulk), mid-term audits (at 50%), and final ex-factory inspection to ISO 2859-1 (AQL 1.5/2.5) before releasing cartons.

  • Independent QC squad stationed on lines
  • Needle detection & metal check logged

Logistics & Customs

We coordinate port delivery via our own fleet and supervise container stuffing. Forwarding bookings are managed with your nominated agents, followed by swift preparation of Bill of Lading, COO, and bank negotiation files.

  • Chittagong seaport & Dhaka airport
  • ETD/ETA milestone tracking
