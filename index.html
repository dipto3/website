Workflow

What We Do?

Step 01

SAMPLING

Rapid proto and development samples, fit correction, and size sets crafted precisely to your technical pack specs.

Step 02

PRODUCTION

Allocation to 15 audited partner factories with daily critical path tracking, fabric booking, and line coordination.

Step 03

QUALITY CONTROL

In-line audits, mid-term inspections, needle detection, lab color continuity, and strict AQL 1.5 final verification.

Step 04

WE DELIVER

Carefully supervised container stuffing at Chittagong Port, swift export documentation, and on-time ex-factory.
