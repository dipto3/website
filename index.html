Workflow

What We Do?

From incoming fabric inspection to precision stitching and on-time global delivery.

Step 01

SAMPLING

Rapid proto and development samples, fit correction, and size sets crafted precisely to technical pack specs.

Step 02

PRODUCTION

Allocation across 15 audited partner factories with daily critical path tracking and inline coordination.

Step 03

QUALITY CONTROL

In-line audits, mid-term inspections, needle detection, lab color continuity, and strict AQL 1.5 final verification.

Step 04

WE DELIVER

Supervised container stuffing at Chittagong Port, swift export documentation, and guaranteed on-time ex-factory.
