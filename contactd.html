Frequently Asked Questions by International Buyers

What are standard payment terms for bulk orders?

We operate via Irrevocable Letter of Credit (Master LC at sight) or Telegraphic Transfer (TT) with standard 30% advance deposit upon sample approval and 70% balance upon submission of export shipping documentation (Bill of Lading).

What is your standard Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

For circular knitwear (T-shirts, polos), our MOQ is typically 500 to 600 pcs per color/style. For heavy woven denim jeans, MOQ is 800 pcs per wash. For 12GG/7GG knit sweaters, MOQ is 350 to 400 pcs. We also accommodate initial trial collection runs for emerging designer labels.

How long does prototype sampling take?

Development Proto samples are produced in our Uttara sample room in 5 to 7 working days upon receiving your tech-pack measurements. Fit samples and Size Sets are dispatched via DHL/FedEx courier with complete measurement deviation sheets.
