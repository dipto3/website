Our Story & Foundation

The Modern Way to Sourcing, Quality Control & Integrity

Founded in 2017 in Uttara, Dhaka, Rsg Appreals Buying House was created with a clear objective: to eradicate the unpredictability, delayed timelines, and opaque pricing that international apparel retailers frequently experienced when sourcing from South Asia.

By building direct operational partnerships with 15 specialized, certified garment manufacturing facilities across Dhaka, Gazipur, and Narayanganj, we provide our buyers with unmatched flexibility: from small-batch boutique trial orders (500 pcs) to high-volume multi-color runs exceeding 500,000 garments.
